# Who Really Am I?

A kid who fell in love with computers.

I'm not someone who had a big social circle growing up. I was the quiet one, few friends, but the ones I have are genuine. That's always been enough for me.

My real journey started right after I finished my +2. I bought my first laptop and that feeling was something else. I installed Kali Linux, broke things constantly, watched YouTube tutorials at 2am, and called my friend over just to get the OS running. I still can't believe that confused kid staring at a terminal is the same person I am today.

Since then, I haven't stopped. I'm currently doing my Bachelor's in CSIT from Nepal, grinding through CTFs, reading security writeups, and going down rabbit holes on HackTheBox and RootMe. Is it boring sometimes? Honestly, yes. But I keep going because this is exactly where I want to be.

I'm an introvert who found his voice through a keyboard.

Right now I'm also learning German because I have a plan. Masters in Cybersecurity in Germany. It's a long road but I'm building toward it one day at a time.

I want to give something back to this world through cybersecurity. I don't know exactly what that looks like yet, but I'm working on it.

Oh and you can call me **rakshak07**. That's my hacking name, given to me by my father. It stuck.
